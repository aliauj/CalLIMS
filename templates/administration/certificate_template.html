Printed above the signature block on every certificate, inside a blue-bordered box. Leave blank to omit the declaration section from the PDF.

Appended after the SHA-256 hash line in the PDF footer.

Where each field appears on the PDF:

  • Lab Name — large heading, top-left of header
  • Lab Subtitle — small text below lab name
  • Accreditation Text — top-right corner
  • Declaration Statement — blue-bordered block above signatures
  • Footer Text — bottom of each page after the hash

Per-certificate Remarks are edited on the individual certificate's Edit Certificate Information page and appear in an amber-bordered block just below the declaration.
